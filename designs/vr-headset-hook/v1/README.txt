VR headset hook (v1)
========================================
Bolts to 40 mm aluminium profile with two M8 T-nuts. A wide, rounded saddle holds the headset by its strap without creasing it.

Print settings
  material: PETG
  printer: Kobra 3
  layer height: 0.2 mm
  walls: 5 walls (2 mm) for strength
  infill: 30% gyroid
  supports: None
  orientation: On its side (the 40 mm wide face down), so the arm's layers run the length of the hook and don't snap off.

Parts
  hook: 75.0 x 40.0 x 60.0 mm, about 36 g

Measurements you can change (ask Claude, or edit model.py)
  Hook width: 40.0 mm
  Arm reach: 75.0 mm
  Back plate height: 60.0 mm
  Arm and plate thickness: 7.0 mm
  End lip height: 22.0 mm
  Bolt hole: 8.5 mm

Before printing
  - Assumes 40 mm aluminium profile and M8 T-nuts. Check what the rig frame uses before printing.

Files: .stl to slice and print, .step to open and edit in Fusion 360 / Onshape / FreeCAD, model.py is the source.
Built 2026-11-07 for the SmartMotion Dashboard.
