Sim button box (v1)
========================================
Two-part box for 6 push buttons, 2 toggle switches and 2 rotary encoders, with a USB port and slots to bolt it to the rig.

Print settings
  material: PETG (PLA+ is fine indoors)
  printer: Kobra 3
  layer height: 0.2 mm
  walls: 4 walls (1.6 mm)
  infill: 20% gyroid
  supports: None: print the body open side up and the lid top face down
  orientation: Body: floor on the bed. Lid: flat face on the bed so the panel comes out smooth.

Parts
  body: 170.0 x 100.0 x 55.0 mm, about 92 g
  lid: 170.0 x 100.0 x 7.0 mm, about 56 g

Measurements you can change (ask Claude, or edit model.py)
  Box width: 170.0 mm
  Box depth: 100.0 mm
  Body height: 55.0 mm
  Wall thickness: 3.0 mm
  Corner radius: 8.0 mm
  Lid thickness: 4.0 mm
  Push button hole: 12.2 mm
  Toggle switch hole: 6.3 mm
  Encoder hole: 7.3 mm
  Heat-set insert hole (M3): 4.0 mm
  Lid lip clearance: 0.3 mm

Before printing
  - Hole sizes assume 12 mm push buttons, MTS-style toggles (6 mm thread) and EC11 encoders (7 mm thread). Measure yours and change the numbers if different.
  - Mounting slots assume M6 bolts. The box's position on the rig hasn't been decided.

Files: .stl to slice and print, .step to open and edit in Fusion 360 / Onshape / FreeCAD, model.py is the source.
Built 2026-11-07 for the SmartMotion Dashboard.
