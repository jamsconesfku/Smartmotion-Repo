Extrusion cable clip (v1)
========================================
Pushes into the 8 mm slot of 40-series aluminium profile and holds a cable bundle up to 10 mm across. No screws needed.

Print settings
  material: PETG (flexes without snapping)
  printer: Kobra 3
  layer height: 0.2 mm
  walls: 3 walls
  infill: 100% (it's small)
  supports: None
  orientation: On its side (the flat 10 mm face on the bed), so the springy legs bend along the layers, not across them.

Parts
  clip: 10.0 x 18.0 x 22.1 mm, about 2 g

Measurements you can change (ask Claude, or edit model.py)
  Profile slot opening: 8.0 mm
  Slot lip thickness: 4.3 mm
  Cable bundle size: 10.0 mm
  Clip thickness: 10.0 mm

Before printing
  - Assumes 40-series profile with an 8 mm slot (common B-type). Measure the slot opening on the rig; if it's 10 mm, change the slot width.

Files: .stl to slice and print, .step to open and edit in Fusion 360 / Onshape / FreeCAD, model.py is the source.
Built 2026-11-07 for the SmartMotion Dashboard.
