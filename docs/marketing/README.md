# Marketing

## Brand and positioning

- Currently presented as "A Skysmart MRO Ltd Company", Great Dunmow, Essex (about 5 minutes from Stansted Airport). Skysmart links to it at https://www.skysmart.co.uk/flight-simulator.
- Tagline: **"Fly it. Drive it. Feel it."**
- Advertise experiences **by type** ("Fast Jet Low-Level", "WW2 Fighter Sortie", "Rally Stage"), never by game or trademarked aircraft names.

## Story assets
- Started as a "bit of fun" on Skysmart's MRO Europe 2024 stand: a "Top Gun" low-level time trial. Aviation Week reported it became a new business line: https://mroevents.aviationweek.com/articles/exhibition-gimmick-spawns-new-business/
- The idea came from a local Scouts visit to Skysmart, where a gliding club set up a basic simulator. Sam spent about five months adding the motion rig and VR.
- Skysmart owns a real ex-RAF **Scottish Aviation Bulldog** trainer: "fly the Bulldog in VR" (F-05).
