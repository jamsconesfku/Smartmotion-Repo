# ADIPS inspection

The **Amusement Device Inspection Procedures Scheme** is the UK industry scheme for rides that move members of the public (HSE guidance **HSG175**).

As a new, self-built device the rig needs:
1. **Design review**
2. **Assessment of conformity to design**
3. **Initial test**

It then gets a **Declaration of Operational Compliance (DOC)**, followed by an **annual in-service inspection**. Event organisers will ask for the DOC.

Rig data the inspector will need (see `docs/product/rig-spec.md`): rated rider load, footprint and exclusion zone, E-stop design, transport weight and size.
