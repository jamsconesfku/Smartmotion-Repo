# Compliance

What SmartMotion needs before taking money, and the rules it works by.

- [ADIPS inspection](adips.md)
- [Insurance](insurance.md)
- [Software licences](software-licences.md)
- Legal paperwork: booking and voucher terms (W4-01), rider declaration and parental consent (W4-02), UK GDPR privacy notice (W4-03).
- Business admin: trading entity (W1-03), trademark check (W1-04), VAT and bank account (W1-05), landlord consent and planning use (W1-06).

## Key point
By **30 Nov 2026** SmartMotion can legally sell **gift vouchers and dated tickets for 2027 sessions**, provided insurance, terms and licences are in place. **No paid rides until the ADIPS DOC is issued.**

## Working principles
- Nothing paid runs on the rig until ADIPS sign-off and insurance are in place.
- Never charge for sessions on consumer-licensed software.
- Advertise experiences by type, not by trademarked aircraft or game names.
- Keep written records of everything: inspections, checks, training, incidents, licences.
